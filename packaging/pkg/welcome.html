This installs VACANCY (the instrument) and VACANCY FX (the effect) by Windward Audio, as Audio Units and VST3 plug-ins, for every user of this Mac. Their recordings and factory presets are inside the plug-ins.

Copies under the old names LIMINAL, TAPEWORN and TAPEWORN FX are removed, and so are copies you installed earlier with install-mac.sh: they are the same plug-ins, and hosts would list them twice. Your songs and your saved presets are kept and open in VACANCY and VACANCY FX.

Quit GarageBand, Logic Pro and any other music app before you continue.
