VACANCY and VACANCY FX are installed.

GarageBand: on a Software Instrument track, open Smart Controls (B), click Plug-ins, then the Instrument slot: AU Instruments > Windward Audio > VACANCY. For the effect, click an empty slot: Audio Units > Windward Audio > VACANCY FX.

Logic Pro: Instrument slot: AU Instruments > Windward Audio > VACANCY; Audio FX slot: Audio Units > Windward Audio > VACANCY FX. If one shows as failed, open Settings > Plug-in Manager and click "Reset & Rescan Selection".

To remove them later: sudo rm -rf "/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/VACANCY.component" "/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/VACANCY FX.component" "/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/VACANCY.vst3" "/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/VACANCY FX.vst3"
