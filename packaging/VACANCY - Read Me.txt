VACANCY by Windward Audio
=========================

VACANCY is a cinematic environmental instrument: twenty instruments played inside
ten half-remembered places. The big LIMINALITY knob sets how unstable, detuned and
half-remembered everything sounds.

VACANCY FX is the same places and LIMINALITY as an audio effect, for any sound:
a voice, a guitar, a synth, drums or a whole mix.

Both are installed by VACANCY.pkg as Audio Unit and VST3 plug-ins.


SYSTEM REQUIREMENTS
  - macOS 10.15 (Catalina) or later
  - Apple Silicon or Intel Mac
  - A host that loads Audio Unit or VST3 plug-ins (GarageBand, Logic Pro,
    Ableton Live, Cubase, Reaper, Studio One, ...)


INSTALL
  1. Quit GarageBand, Logic Pro and any other music apps.
  2. Double-click VACANCY.pkg and follow the steps.
  3. Open your music app. Both plug-ins are listed under Windward Audio.


FINDING IT IN YOUR HOST
  GarageBand
    VACANCY:    create a Software Instrument track, open Smart Controls (B) >
                Plug-ins > Instrument slot > AU Instruments > Windward Audio >
                VACANCY.
    VACANCY FX: on any track, Smart Controls (B) > Plug-ins > an empty slot >
                Audio Units > Windward Audio > VACANCY FX.
    If you see sliders instead of the VACANCY window, switch the plug-in
    window's view menu from "Controls" to "Editor".

  Logic Pro
    VACANCY:    Instrument slot of a Software Instrument track > AU Instruments >
                Windward Audio > VACANCY.
    VACANCY FX: any Audio FX slot > Audio Units > Windward Audio > VACANCY FX.
    If either is missing, open Settings > Plug-in Manager, select it under
    Windward Audio and click "Reset & Rescan Selection".

  Other hosts
    Rescan plug-ins if your host asks, then look for Windward Audio (or
    VACANCY / VACANCY FX) in the instrument or effect list, as AU or VST3.


PRESETS
  The factory presets are built into the plug-ins. Presets you save are kept in:
    ~/Library/Windward Audio/VACANCY/Presets
    ~/Library/Windward Audio/VACANCY FX/Presets
  (~ is your home folder. In Finder, choose Go > Go to Folder and paste the path.)


UNINSTALL
  Quit your music apps, then move these to the Trash (Finder will ask for your
  password):
    /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/VACANCY.component
    /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/VACANCY FX.component
    /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/VACANCY.vst3
    /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/VACANCY FX.vst3
  To remove your saved presets as well, also move these to the Trash:
    ~/Library/Windward Audio/VACANCY
    ~/Library/Windward Audio/VACANCY FX
