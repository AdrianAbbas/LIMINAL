VACANCY by Windward Audio - Cinematic Environmental Instrument
VACANCY FX by Windward Audio - the same places and LIMINALITY, for any sound
(Audio Unit and VST3, macOS 10.15+, Apple Silicon and Intel)

This folder holds both plug-ins. They install side by side and are separate plug-ins
in your host: VACANCY is an instrument (played with MIDI), VACANCY FX is an audio
effect (put it on a guitar, a voice, a synth, drums or a whole mix).

VACANCY is the new name of TAPEWORN (and before that, LIMINAL); VACANCY FX is the
new name of TAPEWORN FX. They are the same plug-ins: songs and presets made with
LIMINAL, TAPEWORN or TAPEWORN FX open in VACANCY and VACANCY FX unchanged. The
installer removes the old copies so your host does not list them twice.

INSTALL
  1. Quit GarageBand and Logic.
  2. Open Terminal (Applications > Utilities > Terminal).
  3. Type "bash " (with a space), drag install-mac.sh from this folder into the
     Terminal window, and press Return.
  4. Wait for "VACANCY and VACANCY FX are installed and validated."

  Why a script? This build is not signed with an Apple Developer ID. When a browser
  downloads it, macOS marks every file as quarantined and then refuses to load the
  plug-in: GarageBand shows a silent slot with plain sliders. The script removes that
  flag, re-signs both plug-ins for this Mac, removes the copies under the old names
  (LIMINAL, TAPEWORN, TAPEWORN FX), refreshes
  the Audio Unit list and runs Apple's validator on both.

GARAGEBAND
  Settings > Audio/MIDI: tick "Audio Units". Restart GarageBand.
  Track > New Track > Software Instrument. Smart Controls (B) > Plug-ins >
  Instrument slot (top) > AU Instruments > Windward Audio > VACANCY.
  The room plays straight away while the track is selected. Play notes (a MIDI
  keyboard, or Window > Show Musical Typing) to hear the Forgotten Piano.
  If you see sliders instead of the VACANCY interface, switch the plug-in
  window's view menu from "Controls" to "Editor".

VACANCY FX IN GARAGEBAND
  Select an audio track (or any track). Smart Controls (B) > Plug-ins > an empty
  slot > Audio Units > Windward Audio > VACANCY FX. Play the track: it is heard in
  the place on screen.

LOGIC PRO
  Settings > Plug-in Manager > Windward Audio > VACANCY and Windward Audio >
  VACANCY FX. If one is marked failed, select it and click "Reset & Rescan
  Selection". VACANCY goes in the Instrument slot of a Software Instrument track
  (AU Instruments > Windward Audio > VACANCY); VACANCY FX in any Audio FX slot
  (Audio Units > Windward Audio > VACANCY FX).

THE WINDOW
  INSTRUMENTS  six slots; each plays any of the twenty instruments (CHOOSE)
  PLACE        ten places, three Characters each
  EFFECTS      each instrument's own effects (the place shapes them: Influence)
  PERFORM      the macros, evolution, scenes A/B, the VHS look
  PRESETS      places, Characters, instruments, scenes and your own presets
  The big LIMINALITY knob (lower left) sets how unstable, detuned and
  half-remembered every instrument is.

VACANCY FX
  The strip    MIX (the input untouched at 0 %, only VACANCY's world at 100 %,
               the default), INTENSITY (how strongly the place transforms the
               sound), INPUT and OUTPUT (dB), SPACE (how much of the room),
               ROOM SOUND (the place's own hum and rare events, only while
               something plays), BYPASS (a short cross-fade, in time).
  The film strip  the ten places; the Character chips under the caption.
  LIMINALITY   the big knob: 0 % clean, 40 % as set on its page, 100 % deeply
               unsettling. Its page: Detune, Drift, Wow & Flutter, Warble, Ghost,
               Wear, Timing, Unevenness, Sour Notes, Fade.
  PLACE        the ten places and their Characters, Space, Room Sound, Far Away,
               the four macros, evolution, the VHS look.
  PRESETS      26 presets by source (vocals, guitar, keys, drums, mix, any) and
               your own; INIT, RESET, UNDO / REDO; Quality.
  A / B        two settings to compare; A>B copies the one shown into the other.
  It reports 20 ms of latency (its pitch line); hosts compensate it, so the
  track stays in time. Every place is level-matched to what comes in.
  Your own presets: ~/Library/Windward Audio/VACANCY FX/Presets (the ones you
  saved with TAPEWORN FX are copied there the first time you open PRESETS).

PRESETS
  INIT, at the top of every list, is "no preset": every instrument, the place,
  the effects and LIMINALITY back to their defaults, as when VACANCY is first
  opened. RESET (beside the preset's name) does the same.
  UNDO / REDO (top right of the drawer) go back and forth between preset loads.
  (Cmd+Z / Cmd+Shift+Z do the same in the standalone app; most hosts keep those
  keys for their own undo.)
  Your own presets are kept in ~/Library/Windward Audio/VACANCY/Presets.
  Presets you saved with TAPEWORN or LIMINAL are copied there the first time you
  open the USER list (the old files stay where they were).

LEVELS
  All twenty instruments play at the same loudness in every place and Character
  (with the place's influence on). With Environment Amount or an instrument's
  Influence at 0 they play at their own dry levels.

UNINSTALL
  bash uninstall-mac.sh            (add --purge to also delete saved presets)
  It removes VACANCY, VACANCY FX and any copy under the old names.

MANUAL INSTALL (if you prefer not to run the script)
  rm -rf ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/LIMINAL.component
  rm -rf ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/TAPEWORN.component
  rm -rf ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/"TAPEWORN FX.component"
  cp -R VACANCY.component ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/
  xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/VACANCY.component
  codesign --force --deep --sign - ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/VACANCY.component
  cp -R "VACANCY FX.component" ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/
  xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/"VACANCY FX.component"
  codesign --force --deep --sign - ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/"VACANCY FX.component"
  killall -9 AudioComponentRegistrar
  auval -v aumu Lmnl Veil
  auval -v aufx Tpfx Wnwd
